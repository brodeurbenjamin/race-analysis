Race Analysis

Line up your Garmin file with your official HYROX splits: pace, heart rate, power and stride for every run, and heart rate for every station.

🔒 Everything happens on this device. Your file and splits are never uploaded or saved.

Garmin Connect → the activity → ⚙ → Export Original. Unzip it if needed.

What stands out

    Runs

    * Left out of the charts: its official time looks like a short course (timing mat layout). It's still in the table. Each run is 1 km, so the official time gives your true pace. The watch misjudges distance indoors by a different amount every run, so its power and step length are corrected to that true pace. Heart rate and cadence come straight from the watch.

    Runs table

    Stations

    Wrist heart rate can't follow arm-heavy or bouncing moves (SkiErg, sled push, burpee broad jumps, lunges); those readings are marked.

    How the watch laps were matched

    Runs are the long laps at running cadence; each station is the longest lap between two runs. Watch laps are button presses, so they differ a little from the official mats.
