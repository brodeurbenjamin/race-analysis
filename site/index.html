Race Analysis

Line up your Garmin file with your official HYROX splits: pace, heart rate, power and stride for every run, and heart rate for every station.

🔒 Everything happens on this device. Your file and splits are never uploaded. If you save a race, only its results (not the file) are kept in this browser, on this device.

Add a race

Garmin Connect → the activity → ⚙ → Export Original. Unzip it if needed.

Official splits

Screenshot your results (the HYROX app's splits list works well; take two if it doesn't fit on one screen, overlapping is fine) and pick them all at once. They're read on this device and never uploaded. Always check the times below.

Or paste the splits as text

Accepted: one segment per line, a name then its time, e.g. Run 1 3:39, SkiErg 00:04:35, Roxzone 6:51, Overall 1:22:03. Names like Ski, BBJ, Burpee Broad Jump, Farmers, Lunges, Wall Balls all work. Times as mm:ss or h:mm:ss. Spaces, tabs or commas between them, so a spreadsheet or CSV copy works too. Anything after the time (a rank, a "PB") is ignored.

    What stands out

      Runs

      * Left out of the charts: its official time looks like a short course (timing mat layout). It's still in the table. Each run is 1 km, so the official time gives your true pace. The watch misjudges distance indoors by a different amount every run, so its power and step length are corrected to that true pace. Heart rate and cadence come straight from the watch.

      Runs table

      Stations

      Wrist heart rate can't follow arm-heavy or bouncing moves (SkiErg, sled push, burpee broad jumps, lunges); those readings are marked.

      How the watch laps were matched

      Runs are the long laps at running cadence; each station is the longest lap between two runs. When a lap press split a segment in two (seen at Boston for the sled push and Run 8), the laps are joined. Watch laps are button presses, so they differ a little from the official mats.
